# Team Lead to Architect

> **Who this is for:** Team leads, technical leads, and principal-track engineers who need to influence system direction across teams.
> **Transition goal:** Move from leading team delivery to shaping architecture, standards, and long-term technical direction.

## What This Role Is Not

Architect is not the person who creates diagrams and leaves implementation to others. Architecture at Techversant should stay close to delivery, risk, maintainability, and team enablement.

## Prerequisites

- Senior to Team Lead expectations consistently met.
- Led multiple major releases or cross-team initiatives.
- Experience documenting technical decisions and trade-offs.
- Evidence of influence beyond one project team.

## Capabilities To Build

### Architecture

- Design scalable and maintainable systems.
- Evaluate technology choices with clear trade-offs.
- Define integration boundaries and API contracts.
- Plan migrations with backward compatibility and rollback.
- Identify security, reliability, and operational risks.

### Strategic Thinking

- Connect technical direction to business outcomes.
- Reduce long-term cost without over-engineering.
- Identify platform and standards opportunities.
- Make architecture decisions understandable to teams and stakeholders.

### Leadership Without Authority

- Influence teams without direct reporting control.
- Mentor senior engineers and team leads.
- Run architecture reviews constructively.
- Create standards that teams can actually adopt.

## Capstone Options

Choose one with the architecture group or engineering leadership:

1. Design and document an end-to-end system architecture with ADRs, risks, and rollout plan.
2. Lead a legacy-system modernization plan with compatibility and migration strategy.
3. Define a technical standard adopted by multiple teams.

## Evidence Checklist

- [ ] I can write an ADR that explains context, options, decision, and consequences.
- [ ] I can defend a design with trade-offs, not preferences.
- [ ] I can identify operational, security, and migration risks.
- [ ] I can guide teams toward a standard without blocking delivery.
- [ ] I can mentor senior engineers on design thinking.
- [ ] I can translate architecture into implementable work.
