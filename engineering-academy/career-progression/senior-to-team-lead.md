# Senior Engineer to Team Lead

> **Who this is for:** Senior engineers who already deliver strong technical work and now need to lead delivery through others.
> **Transition goal:** Move from individual ownership to team-level technical delivery and people development.

## What This Role Is Not

Team Lead is not a senior engineer with more meetings. The role is accountable for clarity, execution, review quality, mentoring, and healthy team flow.

## Prerequisites

- Junior to Senior expectations consistently met.
- Led at least 2 significant features or releases.
- Positive evidence of mentoring, code review quality, and cross-functional collaboration.
- Ability to communicate technical risk to non-specialists.

## Capabilities To Build

### Technical Leadership

- Define a technical plan for a feature or release.
- Resolve blockers without becoming the bottleneck.
- Set review standards and enforce them calmly.
- Guide design decisions with trade-offs and evidence.

### People Leadership

- Run useful 1:1 conversations.
- Coach engineers through growth gaps.
- Give specific feedback early.
- Notice overload, confusion, and hidden blockers.

### Process Leadership

- Improve sprint planning, retrospectives, and delivery flow.
- Coordinate with product, QA, design, and other teams.
- Keep risks visible.
- Protect quality without creating unnecessary process weight.

## Capstone Options

Choose one with your manager:

1. Lead a team through a major release with documented risks, review plan, and rollout notes.
2. Define and apply team engineering standards for one project area.
3. Mentor 2 engineers to independent delivery on scoped work.

## Evidence Checklist

- [ ] I can show a technical plan that helped the team deliver.
- [ ] I can show improved review quality or reduced recurring defects.
- [ ] I can name risks I identified early and how the team handled them.
- [ ] I can show mentoring outcomes, not just mentoring activity.
- [ ] I can run delivery conversations without losing technical depth.
- [ ] I can escalate issues with options, not only problems.
