# Engineering Career Progression

Career progression explains how engineers grow into the next responsibility level. It does not replace HR policy, manager judgment, or project staffing decisions.

Use this section as a capability guide: what to practice, what evidence to collect, what capstone to complete, and what mentor feedback to seek.

## Tracks

| Transition | Guide |
|---|---|
| Junior Engineer to Senior Engineer | [junior-to-senior.md](./junior-to-senior.md) |
| Senior Engineer to Team Lead | [senior-to-team-lead.md](./senior-to-team-lead.md) |
| Team Lead to Architect | [lead-to-architect.md](./lead-to-architect.md) |

## How To Use This Guide

1. Read the guide for your next transition.
2. Map your current evidence against the readiness checklist.
3. Pick one capstone with your mentor or manager.
4. Use the relevant academy paths to close gaps.
5. Review progress monthly.

## Required Baseline For All Transitions

- [Engineering Foundations](../00-engineering-foundations/README.md) completed or equivalent evidence.
- [Developer Excellence Curriculum](../00-engineering-foundations/dev-excellence/curriculum.md) completed or in progress.
- Clear PR history with review participation.
- Capstone project completed.
- Mentor or manager sign-off.
