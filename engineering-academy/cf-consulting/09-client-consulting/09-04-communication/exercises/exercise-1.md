# Exercise 1: Language Reframing

> Practice using confident, professional language instead of weak hedging.

---

## Scenario

Rewrite these phrases to project confidence:

### Before/After

| Weak Phrase | Confident Alternative |
|-------------|----------------------|
| "I think we should..." | "Based on our analysis, I recommend..." |
| "Maybe we could..." | "One approach would be to..." |
| "I don't know if..." | "Let me verify that and follow up." |
| "That might be a problem." | "The main challenge I see is..." |
| "We usually do it this way." | "A proven approach for this situation is..." |
