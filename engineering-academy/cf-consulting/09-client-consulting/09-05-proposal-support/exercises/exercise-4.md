# Exercise 4: Proposal Review and Quality Check

> Review a proposal section for clarity, completeness, and professional tone.

---

## Scenario

Review this (hypothetical) scope section for a BlogCFC5 modernization proposal and identify issues.

---

## Sample Scope Section (With Issues)

```
SCOPE OF WORK

1.0 PROJECT OVERVIEW
We will migrate your ColdFusion application to Lucee. This includes
looking at the code, fixing things, and deploying it to the cloud.

2.0 DELIVERABLES
- Updated application
- Documentation
- Training

3.0 TIMELINE
The project will take about 6 weeks. Maybe 8 if things go wrong.

4.0 ASSUMPTIONS
The client will help us when we need it.
```

---

## Issues to Find

| Category | Issue | Suggested Fix |
|----------|-------|---------------|
| Clarity | "looking at the code, fixing things" is vague | Be specific: "Compatibility scan, fixes, testing" |
| Timeline | "Maybe 8 weeks" sounds uncertain | Use range: "6-8 weeks depending on findings" |
| Assumptions | "Help us when we need it" is unclear | Specify: "1-2 hour response within business hours" |
