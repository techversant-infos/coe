## Progression

| Contribution Level | What to Demonstrate |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| Contributor | Contribute to discovery, estimate, or proposal section with review |
| Supported Lead | Lead discovery or presentation with peer review |
| Lead | Own client relationships; mentor others |

Use the [capstone/mock-clients](../mock-clients/) for practice if you need evidence before a real engagement.
