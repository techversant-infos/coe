# Evidence-Based Completion Framework

> Observable evidence requirements for CoE certification.

---

## Purpose

Competency scores alone are subjective. This framework defines **observable evidence** — concrete artifacts and demonstrations that prove each competency.

---

## Completion Requirements

To complete the CF Consulting CoE, developers must produce evidence across three categories:

| Category | Required Evidence | Weight |
|----------|------------------|--------|
| Artifacts | Documents produced | 40% |
| Demonstrations | Live performances | 40% |
| Reviews | Peer feedback | 20% |

### Client Skills

#### Discovery Leadership

**Required Evidence:**
- [ ] **Recorded discovery session** (min 30 minutes)
- [ ] Discovery questionnaire completed for a mock client
- [ ] Follow-up questions documented

**Verification:** Recording reviewed by Practice Lead

**Scoring Rubric:**

| Score | Observation |
|-------|-------------|
| 1-3 | Hesitant, let client dominate, missed key questions |
| 4-5 | Covered basics, some awkward pauses, unclear follow-ups |
| 6-7 | Good flow, appropriate questions, natural follow-ups |
| 8-9 | Confident, probing questions, client engaged |
| 10 | Expert facilitation, insights surfaced, next steps clear |

## Scoring Formula

### Final Score Calculation

Both competency scores (0-10 scale) and evidence scores (0-100 scale) are normalized to a 0-100% scale before combining:

```
Final Score = (Competency % × 0.5) + (Evidence % × 0.5)
```

**Where:**
- **Competency %** = (Competency Average / 10) × 100
- **Evidence %** = Weighted evidence completion (0-100)

### Evidence Score Calculation

| Evidence Category | Weight | Calculation |
|-------------------|--------|-------------|
| Artifacts | 40% | (Items completed / Required items) × 100 |
| Demonstrations | 40% | (Items completed / Required items) × 100 |
| Reviews | 20% | (Reviews contributed / 3 minimum) × 100 |

**Evidence Score Formula:**
```
Evidence % = (Artifacts% × 0.4) + (Demonstrations% × 0.4) + (Reviews% × 0.2)
```

### Example Calculation

| Component | Raw Score | Converted |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Competency Average | 7.5 / 10 | 75% |
| Artifacts Completed | 8 of 10 | 80% |
| Demonstrations Completed | 4 of 5 | 80% |
| Reviews Contributed | 3 of 3 | 100% |

**Evidence %:**
```
(80 × 0.4) + (80 × 0.4) + (100 × 0.2) = 32 + 32 + 20 = 84%
```

**Final Score:**
```
(75 × 0.5) + (84 × 0.5) = 37.5 + 42 = 79.5 → 80%
```

### Certification Thresholds

| Final Score | Decision | Next Steps |
|-------------|----------|------------|
| **≥ 70%** | **Certified** | Receive certificate |
| **60-69%** | **Provisional** | Complete remediation items within 30 days |
| **< 60%** | **Not Certified** | Repeat failed phases, reassess after 90 days |

### Competency Minimums

Even with high evidence scores, you must meet minimum competency thresholds:

| Category | Minimum Average | Notes |
|----------|-----------------|-------|
| Technical Skills | 6/10 | Each technical area ≥ 5/10 |
| Client Skills | 6/10 | Discovery and presentation ≥ 6/10 |
| Behaviour | 5/10 | No critical flags |

### Remediation Process

For **Provisional** status:

1. Review feedback from assessor
2. Identify specific gaps (Evidence Framework → Competency Gap Analysis)
3. Create remediation plan (max 30 days)
4. Submit updated evidence
5. Resubmit for final assessment

### Appeal Process

If you disagree with the certification decision:

1. Submit written appeal within 14 days
2. Practice Lead reviews evidence independently
3. Second assessment conducted if warranted
4. Final decision by Practice Director

## Peer Review Protocol

### How Peer Reviews Work

1. Submit your work for peer review
2. Complete 2 peer reviews of others' work
3. Receive feedback from 2 peers
4. Incorporate feedback and resubmit if needed
