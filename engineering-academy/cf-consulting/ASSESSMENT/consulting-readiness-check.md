# Consulting Readiness Check

## How to Use This

For each question, choose the answer that best fits your current situation:

- **Ready** — You have evidence of this capability
- **Ready with support** — You can do this with peer review or coaching
- **Build confidence** — One targeted activity will help
- **Not pursuing** — Not relevant to your chosen pathway
