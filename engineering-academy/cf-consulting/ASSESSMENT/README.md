# Assessment Framework

> Measure and track each developer's journey from ColdFusion developer to technical consultant.

## Purpose

The assessment framework ensures:

- **Baseline measurement** before training begins
- **Targeted learning** based on identified gaps
- **Progress tracking** through monthly reviews
- **Accountability** for both developer and manager
- **Program effectiveness** measurement

## Three Competency Areas

| Area | Weight | Focus |
|------|--------|-------|
| Technical Skills | 50% | CF-specific and adjacent technical knowledge |
| Client Skills | 30% | Consulting, communication, and presentation |
| Behaviour | 20% | Personal attributes for client-facing work |

## Assessment Cycle

| Phase | Timing | Purpose | Output |
|-------|--------|---------|--------|
| Initial Assessment | Week 1 | Baseline measurement | Competency matrix scores |
| Individual Plans | Week 1–2 | Gap analysis and planning | IDP document |
| Monthly Check-in | Every 30 days | Progress tracking | Updated tracker |
| Quarterly Review | Month 3 | Competency milestone | Review summary |
| Final Assessment | Month 6 | Program completion | Final scores + sign-off |

## Success Thresholds

| Competency Area | Entry Target | Monthly Progress | Completion Target |
|----------------|--------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Technical Skills | 4/10 average | +0.5/month | 7/10 average |
| Client Skills | 3/10 average | +0.5/month | 6/10 average |
| Behaviour | 4/10 average | +0.3/month | 6/10 average |

Individual targets are set in each developer's IDP based on:

- Current role and experience level
- Priority gaps identified in initial assessment
- Business needs and client engagement focus

## Scoring Consistency

To ensure fair assessments across all developers:

1. **Use concrete examples** — Don't score based on impression. Reference specific evidence.
2. **Calibrate with the guide** — Read the [Assessor Guide](./resources/assessor-guide.md) before conducting assessments.
3. **Multiple perspectives** — Combine self-assessment with manager observation.
4. **Trend over snapshots** — Look at trajectory, not just current state.
5. **Discuss discrepancies** — If self-score and manager score differ by 2+, discuss and align.

## Assessment Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Why It's a Problem | Fix |
|--------------|-------------------|-----|
| All 5s and 6s | Avoids difficult conversations | Use the full scale. 5 = average developer. |
| Recent event bias | Last week affects all scores | Consider entire period |
| Halo effect | Strong in one area inflates all | Score each skill independently |
| Harsh on juniors | Unfair comparison to seniors | Use role-appropriate targets |
