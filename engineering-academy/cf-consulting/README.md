# ColdFusion Consulting Practice

> Build consulting capability aligned with your experience and career goals.

---

## What Is This?

A self-directed practice initiative for ColdFusion developers and architects. Use it to:

- Activate consulting skills you already have
- Deepen specific capabilities where needed
- Contribute to real client engagements at your readiness level

**Participation is role-aligned and voluntary.** Choose only the pathways relevant to your goals.

---

## Contribution Levels

| Level | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| **Observer** | Shadow client calls and reviews; no client contact required |
| **Contributor** | Contribute to assessments, estimates, or deliverables with support |
| **Supported Lead** | Lead discovery sessions or presentations with peer review |
| **Lead** | Own client relationships; present findings; mentor others |

Your level may differ by pathway. A performance specialist can Lead on technical work while Contributing on client-facing work.

---

## Consulting Pathways

| Pathway | Best For | Primary Level |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| **Technical Assessor** | Analysts and reviewers | Contributor → Supported Lead |
| **Modernization Architect** | Solution design and strategy | Contributor → Lead |
| **Lucee Specialist** | Migration planning and execution | Contributor → Lead |
| **Cloud Specialist** | Infrastructure and deployment | Contributor → Lead |
| **Performance Specialist** | Optimization and reliability | Contributor → Lead |
| **UI/API Specialist** | Frontend and integration modernization | Contributor → Lead |
| **AI Integration Advisor** | AI opportunities and implementation | Contributor → Supported Lead |
| **Client-Facing Lead** | End-to-end client relationships | Supported Lead → Lead |

Specialists and client-facing leads can coexist. A strong specialist does not need to become a sales-oriented consultant.
