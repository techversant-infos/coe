# Exercise 2: Business Case Development

> Build a business case for ColdFusion modernization.

## Objective

Learn to quantify the value of modernization in business terms.

## Scenario

**Client:** Metro Logistics (from Exercise 1)
**Current State:** CF 10, 180 files, frequent security patches needed
**Issue:** Team spends 30% of time on maintenance, can't add new features

## Instructions

### Part 1: Quantify the Problem

**Current Annual Costs:**

| Cost Item | Annual Amount | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Security patching | $12,000 | Emergency patches, developer time |
| Bug fixes | $45,000 | 2 devs × 20% time |
| Downtime incidents | $8,000 | 4 incidents × $2k each |
| Lost productivity | $25,000 | Slow development velocity |
| Risk of breach | $50,000 | Estimated uninsured risk |
| **Total Current Cost** | **$140,000** | |

### Part 4: Calculate ROI

**ROI Formula:**
```
ROI = (Total Benefits - Total Investment) / Total Investment × 100
```

**Your Calculation:**
```
Total Investment: $175,000
Total Benefits (3 years): $345,000
Net Benefit: $170,000

ROI = ___________________________________%
```
