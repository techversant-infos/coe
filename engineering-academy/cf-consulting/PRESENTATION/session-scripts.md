# Practice Rollout Scripts

> Talking points for team sessions.

---

## Session 1: The Opportunity (15 min)

### Opening (2 min)

> "I want to share something we're starting that I think creates real opportunity for all of us."
>
> "Techversant is building a ColdFusion consulting practice for the US market. That means helping US companies modernize and maintain their ColdFusion applications."
>
> "Here's why this matters to you: your ColdFusion experience is expertise. This practice initiative helps you activate that expertise in client-facing work—on your timeline, at your level."

### The Market Opportunity (4 min)

> "US companies still run ColdFusion. Many are on old versions, have security concerns, want cloud but don't know how."
>
> "They need help. We want to be the team they call."
>
> "This isn't about becoming a salesperson. It's about being a more effective technical professional who can translate expertise into client value."

### Benefits to the Team (4 min)

> "How you participate is up to you."
>
> "You might contribute as:
> - A technical specialist who reviews and advises on architecture
> - A migration expert who plans and executes Lucee moves
> - A client-facing consultant who builds relationships and guides decisions
>
> "All of these contribute to the practice. All of them are valuable."

### Addressing Concerns (4 min)

> "Some of you might think: 'I'm a developer, not a consultant.' That's fine. Specialists are essential."
>
> "Others might think: 'I don't want to lead client meetings.' Also fine. You can contribute as an observer or contributor."
>
> "This isn't about forcing everyone into the same mold. It's about creating pathways for different strengths."

### Close (1 min)

> "Take a look at the [one-pager](./one-pager.md) before our next session. And come with questions about what pathway might suit you."

## Session 2: Pathways and Roles (20 min)

### Pathway Overview (10 min)

Walk through the pathway table:

> "Here are the main ways you can contribute:"

| Pathway | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Technical Assessor | Review legacy applications; produce structured assessments |
| Modernization Architect | Design target architectures and modernization strategies |
| Lucee Specialist | Plan and execute Lucee migrations |
| Cloud Specialist | Design cloud architectures for CF workloads |
| Performance Specialist | Profile, diagnose, and optimize CF applications |
| UI/API Specialist | Modernize interfaces and build CF APIs |
| AI Integration Advisor | Identify and implement AI opportunities |
| Client-Facing Lead | Own client relationships end-to-end |

> "You don't need to pick one and stick with it forever. Many people have a technical specialty and add consulting skills over time."

### Contribution Levels (5 min)

> "Here's how contribution progresses:"

| Level | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| Observer | Shadow calls and reviews; learn without direct client contact |
| Contributor | Contribute to assessments, estimates, or deliverables with support |
| Supported Lead | Lead sessions with peer review |
| Lead | Own relationships; mentor others |

> "You might be a Lead on technical work while a Contributor on client-facing work. That's fine."

### Self-Assessment Preview (5 min)

> "Before our next session, complete the [readiness check](../ASSESSMENT/consulting-readiness-check.md). It's about 20 questions."
>
> "The goal is not to pass. It's to identify where you already contribute and where you might want to deepen."

## Session 3: How It Works (15 min)

### Getting Started (5 min)

> "Here's how to get started:"

1. Complete the readiness check (20 questions)
2. Select your primary pathway
3. Document existing evidence
4. Choose one targeted activity if needed
5. Agree next contribution opportunity with practice lead

> "No fixed timeline. No mandatory phases. Just focus on the gap that matters most."

### Resources Available (5 min)

> "Here are the resources:"

| Resource | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| [Readiness check](../ASSESSMENT/consulting-readiness-check.md) | Identify gaps and strengths |
| [90-day plan](../ASSESSMENT/90-day-plan.md) | Structure your growth |
| [Client communication guide](../09-client-consulting/client-communication-guide.md) | Practical guidance |
| [Phase modules](../) | Detailed technical content |
| [Capstone](../capstone/) | Safe practice environment |
| [Operating model](../10-consulting-operating-model/) | How engagements work |

### Real Work Takes Priority (3 min)

> "This supplements your delivery work, not replaces it."
>
> "When you have capacity, use the resources. When you're on a tight deadline, focus on delivery."
>
> "Development time must be practical alongside active client commitments."

## Session 4: Individual Discussions (30 min per person)

### Per-Person Discussion

1. **Share their readiness check results** (or discuss their pathway interest)
2. **Identify one growth area** that matters for their goals
3. **Agree one immediate action** (targeted activity or real-work opportunity)
4. **Set a review date** (suggest 4-6 weeks)

### Sample Conversation

> "Based on your experience, I think [Technical Assessor] or [Modernization Architect] might be a good fit. What do you think?"
>
> "Looking at your readiness check, you marked Q2 and Q4 as 'Build confidence.' Want to focus on one of those?"
>
> "For this week, could you [review one exercise] or [shadow the next discovery call]?"
>
> "Let's catch up in 4 weeks to see how that went."

---

## Key Phrases to Use

### Instead of "Program"
Use: practice, initiative, pathway, contribution

### Instead of "Assignment"
Use: targeted activity, growth area, one action

### Instead of "Complete all phases"
Use: choose your pathway, deepen where you want to grow

### Instead of "Mandatory"
Use: role-aligned, voluntary, self-directed

### Instead of "Training"
Use: practice, development, capability building
