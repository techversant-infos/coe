# Practice Rollout Guide

> How to introduce the ColdFusion Consulting Practice to the team.

---

## What This Is

A self-directed practice initiative—not a training program. Developers use it to activate, validate, and deepen consulting capability aligned with their experience and career goals.

---

## Key Messages for the Team

### This Is Voluntary and Role-Aligned

> "This is not mandatory coursework. It's a set of resources you can use to develop consulting skills relevant to your role and goals."

### Different People Contribute Differently

> "Some of you will contribute as technical specialists. Others will lead client relationships. Both are valuable."

### Progression Is Flexible

> "You can move from Observer to Contributor to Supported Lead to Lead over time. There's no fixed timeline."

### Real Work Takes Priority

> "This supplements your active delivery work. Use it when you have capacity, not instead of client commitments."

## Session 1: The Opportunity

### Talking Points

1. **Market need:** US companies run ColdFusion and need modernization help.
2. **Your expertise:** 5-18 years of experience is valuable expertise.
3. **Career pathway:** Consulting skills create new opportunities.

### Key Message

> "Your ColdFusion expertise has value beyond our current projects. This practice initiative helps you activate that expertise in client-facing work—on your timeline and at your level."

---

## Session 2: Pathways and Roles

### Pathways to Present

| Pathway | What They Do |
|---------|-------------|
| Technical Assessor | Review legacy systems, produce assessment reports |
| Modernization Architect | Design solution strategies and roadmaps |
| Lucee Specialist | Plan and execute Lucee migrations |
| Cloud Specialist | Design cloud architectures and migrations |
| Performance Specialist | Profile and optimize application performance |
| UI/API Specialist | Modernize frontends and expose APIs |
| AI Integration Advisor | Identify and implement AI opportunities |
| Client-Facing Lead | Own end-to-end client relationships |

### Contribution Levels

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Observer | Shadow calls, review deliverables |
| Contributor | Add to assessments with peer support |
| Supported Lead | Lead workshops with peer review |
| Lead | Own relationships, present findings |

### Key Message

> "You don't need to become a sales person to contribute. A strong technical specialist who can review a legacy app or estimate migration effort contributes real value."

---

## Session 3: How It Works

### Starting Point

1. Complete the [Readiness Check](../ASSESSMENT/consulting-readiness-check.md)
2. Select your pathway
3. Submit existing evidence or choose one targeted activity
4. Align on next contribution opportunity

### Time Commitment

> "This is designed to fit around active delivery. There's no mandatory weekly homework. You choose what to deepen based on your gaps and goals."

### Support Structure

- Peer reviews for deliverables
- Co-delivery opportunities for new contributors
- Practice sessions for discovery and presentation
- Optional capstone for those who want safe practice

---

## Session 4: Individual Discussion

### Per-Developer Conversation

For each developer, discuss:

1. **Current role and interests** — What kind of consulting work appeals?
2. **Existing evidence** — What can they submit from past work?
3. **Gaps to address** — What does the readiness check suggest?
4. **Next action** — One targeted activity for the next 30 days

### Outcome

- [ ] Pathway selected
- [ ] Current contribution level agreed
- [ ] One growth goal identified
- [ ] Next check-in scheduled
