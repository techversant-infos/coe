# CF Consulting Practice — One Page Overview

> A self-directed initiative for experienced ColdFusion developers.

---

## What Is This?

A practice initiative that helps you activate and deepen consulting capability:

- Use your CF expertise in client-facing work
- Choose pathways that match your goals
- Contribute at your readiness level

**Participation is voluntary and role-aligned.** No mandatory coursework.

---

## Contribution Levels

| Level | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| **Observer** | Shadow client calls and reviews |
| **Contributor** | Contribute to assessments, estimates with support |
| **Supported Lead** | Lead discovery or presentations with peer review |
| **Lead** | Own client relationships; mentor others |

---

## Consulting Pathways

Choose one or two based on your goals:

| Pathway | What They Do |
|---------|-------------|
| **Technical Assessor** | Review and document legacy systems |
| **Modernization Architect** | Design upgrade/migration strategies |
| **Lucee Specialist** | Plan and execute Lucee migrations |
| **Cloud Specialist** | Design cloud architectures for CF |
| **Performance Specialist** | Diagnose and optimize slow systems |
| **UI/API Specialist** | Modernize interfaces and APIs |
| **AI Integration Advisor** | Scope AI opportunities |
| **Client-Facing Lead** | End-to-end client relationships |

A strong specialist does not need to become a sales-oriented consultant.

---

## How It Works

1. **Read this overview**
2. **Complete the readiness check** (20 questions, 15 minutes)
3. **Select your pathway**
4. **Create a 90-day plan** (1-3 goals)
5. **Use existing evidence or choose one targeted activity**
6. **Agree next contribution opportunity**

Real work takes priority over coursework.

---

## Evidence Over Coursework

Your existing work counts:

- Assessment reports you've written
- Estimates you've produced
- Presentations you've delivered
- Code reviews you've conducted

Submit existing evidence. You may already be at Contributor level.
