# Choose Your Consulting Pathway

> 30-minute self-directed start for experienced ColdFusion developers.

---

## Step 1: Read the Practice Charter (5 min)

[PRESENTATION/one-pager.md](./PRESENTATION/one-pager.md)

Understand what the consulting practice is and how participation works.

---

## Step 2: Complete the Readiness Check (15 min)

[ASSESSMENT/consulting-readiness-check.md](./ASSESSMENT/consulting-readiness-check.md)

For each question, select:
- **Ready** — You have evidence
- **Ready with support** — You can do this with peer review
- **Build confidence** — One targeted activity will help
- **Not pursuing** — Not relevant to your pathway

---

## Step 3: Select Your Pathway (2 min)

Based on your readiness check, choose your primary pathway:

| Pathway | When to Choose |
|---------|----------------|
| [Technical Assessor](02-legacy-assessment/) | Reviewing and documenting legacy systems |
| [Modernization Architect](03-modernization-strategy/) | Recommending strategic direction |
| [Lucee Specialist](04-lucee-migration/) | Planning Lucee migrations |
| [Cloud Specialist](05-cloud-migration/) | Moving workloads to AWS/Azure |
| [Performance Specialist](08-performance-engineering/) | Optimizing slow systems |
| [UI/API Specialist](07-ui-modernization/) | Modernizing interfaces and APIs |
| [AI Integration Advisor](06-ai-integration/) | Adding AI capabilities |
| [Client-Facing Lead](09-client-consulting/) | End-to-end client relationships |

You do not need to choose a single pathway. Most people have a primary technical specialty and develop consulting skills around it.

---

## Step 4: Review Your Evidence (5 min)

Before starting any new learning, document what you already have:

| Evidence Type | What to Submit |
|---------------|----------------|
| Assessment reports | Existing code review or assessment documents |
| Discovery notes | Meeting notes from requirements gathering |
| Estimates | Any previous effort estimates you've produced |
| Architecture work | Diagrams, decision records, proposal sections |
| Presentations | Decks you've presented to stakeholders |

Submit existing evidence to your practice lead. You may already be at Contributor level.

---

## Step 5: Choose One Targeted Activity (3 min)

If you have gaps:

1. Return to your readiness check
2. Find your "Build confidence" answers
3. Pick **one** that matters most for your pathway
4. Complete that activity before moving to another

**Do not try to do everything at once.**

---

## Step 6: Agree Your Next Contribution

Work with your practice lead to identify a real opportunity:

- **Observer** — Shadow a client call or review
- **Contributor** — Contribute to an assessment or estimate
- **Supported Lead** — Lead a section with peer review
- **Lead** — Own a client interaction

---

## Capstone: Safe Practice Environment

The [capstone](./capstone/README.md) provides BlogCFC5 — a real ColdFusion application — for practice when:

- You need evidence before contributing to a client engagement
- You want to practice in a safe environment
- You prefer to build confidence before real exposure

The capstone is **optional**, not mandatory.
