# 05. Engineering Leadership

Engineering Leadership is a planned academy discipline for engineers who lead delivery through people, systems, standards, and communication.

This section should cover technical leadership and people leadership without confusing it with HR policy.

## Intended Scope

- Team lead fundamentals
- Technical leadership
- Engineering management basics
- Mentoring and coaching
- Delivery ownership
- Stakeholder communication
- Code review culture
- Team standards and operating rhythms
- Planning, estimation, and risk management
- Feedback, 1:1s, and growth plans
- Conflict handling and decision facilitation
- Hiring and onboarding basics

## Recommended Future Order

1. Team Lead Fundamentals — clarify expectations before someone becomes the bottleneck
2. Mentoring and Coaching — turn senior experience into repeatable team growth
3. Delivery Leadership — covers scope, risk, trade-offs, and release readiness
4. Review Culture — protect quality without creating process drag
5. Engineering Management Basics — 1:1s, feedback, staffing, performance signals, and escalation
6. Cross-functional Communication — work with product, QA, design, support, and stakeholders
