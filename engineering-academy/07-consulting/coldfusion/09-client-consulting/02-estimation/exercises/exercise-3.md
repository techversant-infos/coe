# Exercise 3: Estimate Presentation

> Practice presenting an estimate to a client with appropriate confidence levels.

---

## Scenario

You have completed an estimate for the BlogCFC5 modernization. Now you need to present it to the client (the CTO).

**Time limit:** 10 minutes

---

## Exercise 3A: Structure Your Presentation

### Opening (1 min)

> "Based on our discovery, here's what we found and what it means for timeline and investment."

### Estimate Summary (4 min)

**Present the numbers:**
- Low estimate: X hours
- Likely estimate: Y hours
- High estimate: Z hours

**Explain the range:**
> "The range accounts for unknowns we identified during discovery. The likely estimate assumes we find no major surprises."

### Key Assumptions (2 min)

| Assumption | Impact if Wrong |
|------------|-----------------|
| No major custom auth rewrites | +20% effort |
| Database schema is documented | -10% effort |
| Codebase is mostly structured | Standard range |

### Risk Buffer (1 min)

> "We've included a 15% buffer for unknowns identified during discovery."

### Close (2 min)

> "This estimate is valid for 30 days. Once we begin work, we'll refine as we learn more."
