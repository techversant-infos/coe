# Exercise 4: Difficult Questions and Objection Handling

> Practice responding to challenging audience questions with confidence.

---

## Scenario

You are presenting the BlogCFC5 modernization proposal. The audience asks difficult questions.

---

## Question 1: "This sounds expensive."

**Bad response:**
> "Well, it's not that expensive considering..."
> (Sounds defensive)

**Better response:**
> "I understand budget is a concern. What we're proposing is an investment that reduces ongoing maintenance costs and risk. The total cost of staying on the current platform—including security monitoring, compatibility issues, and limited capability—is often higher over a 3-year horizon. Would it help if I walked through the cost comparison?"

---

## Question 2: "We've been told ColdFusion is dying."

**Bad response:**
> "ColdFusion isn't dying, that's just perception..."
> (Sounds defensive)

**Better response:**
> "That's a common concern, and I understand where it's coming from. Adobe continues to release ColdFusion, and Lucee provides an active open-source path. What we're seeing is not a death spiral but a consolidation. Companies are choosing to either invest in their CF systems or migrate. Both paths are viable. The question is which fits your situation."

---

## Question 3: "Can you guarantee the timeline?"

**Bad response:**
> "Yes, we'll hit the deadline."
> (Overcommitment)

**Better response:**
> "I can't guarantee an exact date without completing discovery, but I can tell you what affects timeline: scope clarity, feedback speed, and testing availability. If those are addressed, our estimates are typically within 15% of actual. What I can commit to is weekly updates and proactive communication if anything changes."

---

## Question 4: "Why should we trust your team?"

**Better response:**
> "That's the right question to ask. A few things set us apart: we've been working with ColdFusion since version 4, we've completed X similar migrations, and we back our work with a warranty period. I can share references if that would help."
