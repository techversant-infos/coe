# Exercise 3: Full Presentation Delivery

> Deliver a complete 15-minute presentation to an audience and receive feedback.

---

## Scenario

Present the BlogCFC5 modernization findings to the Pinnacle Regional Health System leadership team.

**Audience:** CTO, VP of Operations, IT Director
**Time:** 15 minutes + 10 minutes Q&A

---

## Presentation Structure

### Slide 1: Title (30 sec)

```
Modernization Assessment: BlogCFC5
Prepared for: Pinnacle Regional Health System
Date: [Today]
```

### Slide 2: Agenda (30 sec)

```
1. Executive Summary
2. Current State Assessment
3. Risks and Opportunities
4. Recommended Path Forward
5. Investment and Timeline
6. Next Steps
```

### Slide 3: Executive Summary (1 min)

**Key message:** Your BlogCFC5 application works but carries technical debt that creates risk.

### Slide 4: Current State (3 min)

- What we reviewed
- Key findings
- Risk summary

### Slide 5: Technical Details (3 min)

- Architecture overview
- Key issues identified
- Security findings

### Slide 6: Options (3 min)

| Option | Investment | Timeline | Risk |
|--------|-----------|----------|------|
| Stay | $X/mo | Ongoing | High |
| Upgrade | $$ | Y months | Medium |
| Migrate | $$$ | Z months | Low |

### Slide 7: Recommendation (2 min)

**Our recommendation:** [Explain your choice]

### Slide 8: Investment (1 min)

| Phase | Hours | Timeline |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Discovery | X | Y weeks |
| Migration | X | Y weeks |
| Testing | X | Y weeks |

### Slide 9: Next Steps (1 min)

1. Decision on approach
2. Contract preparation
3. Kickoff planning

---

## Practice Tips

- **Start strong:** Your opening sets the tone
- **Business first:** Lead with business impact
- **Visual aids:** Use diagrams, not walls of text
- **Pace yourself:** 2 minutes per slide maximum
- **End with action:** Tell them what you need from them

---

## Deliverable

- Completed slide deck
- Recorded presentation (or peer review)
- Feedback received
