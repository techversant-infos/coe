# Exercise 1: Present Your Assessment

> Practice presenting technical findings to a non-technical audience.

---

## Scenario

You've completed a legacy assessment for BlogCFC5. Now you need to present findings to the client (a CTO with limited technical background).

**Time limit:** 10 minutes

---

## Exercise 1A: Structure Your Presentation

Create a presentation structure:

```
Slide 1: Title
  Title: _______________________________________________

Slide 2: Executive Summary
  - Key finding 1
  - Key finding 2
  - Recommendation

Slide 3: Current State
  - What we reviewed
  - Key metrics

Slide 4: Findings
  - Risk 1: _______________
  - Risk 2: _______________
  - Risk 3: _______________

Slide 5: Options
  - Option A: _______________
  - Option B: _______________

Slide 6: Recommendation
  - Why: _______________
  - Investment: _______________
  - Timeline: _______________

Slide 7: Next Steps
  - Step 1: _______________
  - Step 2: _______________
```

---

## Exercise 1B: Practice

Record yourself presenting (use your phone). Time yourself.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Under 10 minutes
- [ ] No jargon
- [ ] Used analogies
- [ ] Made eye contact (or looked at camera)
- [ ] Ended with clear next steps
