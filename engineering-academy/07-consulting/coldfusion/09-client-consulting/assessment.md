## Answer Key

### Section A

**A1 - Suggested Top 10 Questions:**
- What version of CF are you running? (Critical: CF2018 is current)
- Are you using a framework? (80 files suggests possible structure)
- How many users? (Performance context)
- What are the specific performance pain points?
- What's the database size?
- What integrations exist?
- What's your cloud experience?
- What's your timeline expectation?
- What's your budget?
- Who are the decision makers?

**A2 - Workshop Flow:**
- Opening (15): Introduce, set agenda
- Current State (30): Technical deep dive
- Pain Points (30): Performance focus
- Future Vision (15): What they want to achieve
- Solution Options (15): Cloud vs upgrade
- Close (15): Next steps

**Risks:** Client unprepared, scope creep, technical gatekeeper absent

### Section B

**B1 - Transformations:**
- "Based on our experience..."
- "A viable approach would be..."
- "Let me research that and provide a recommendation..."
- "We've found different approaches work better in similar situations..."
- "We've implemented similar solutions. Here's how we might approach yours..."

**B2 - Response Framework:**
- Acknowledge the goal
- Identify reality (2 weeks is extremely aggressive)
- Bridge to realistic timeline
- Offer phased approach

### Section C

**C1 - Estimates:**
- Assessment: 40-80 hours typical
- Migration: 160-400 hours typical (depending on scope)
- Buffer: Essential

**C2 - Scope:**
Should include specific deliverables, timelines, and success criteria.

**C3 - Assumptions:**
- Team availability
- Environment access
- Decision-making timeline
- Current code quality
- User involvement

### Section D

**D1 - Problems:**
- Vague deliverables
- No specific scope
- Undefined timeline
- Informal language
- No success criteria
- No risk acknowledgment

**D2 - Improved Version:**
Should be specific, professional, with clear deliverables and timeline.
