# Exercise 3: Mock Discovery Session

> Lead a 2-hour discovery workshop with a peer playing the client role.

---

## Setup

**Role A:** Consultant (you)
**Role B:** Client (peer)

**Scenario:** See [capstone/pinnacle-scenario.md](../../capstone/pinnacle-scenario.md)

---

## Before the Session

### Prepare Your Agenda

```
Discovery Workshop Agenda (2 hours)

0:00 - Welcome and introductions (5 min)
0:05 - Business context (15 min)
0:20 - Current application overview (20 min)
0:40 - Pain points and issues (20 min)
1:00 - Break (5 min)
1:05 - Technical deep-dive (20 min)
1:25 - Future vision and goals (15 min)
1:40 - Constraints and timeline (10 min)
1:50 - Summary and next steps (10 min)
```

### Prepare Your Questions

| Section | Questions to Ask |
|---------|-----------------|
| Business | "What business problem are you solving?" |
| | "Who are the end users?" |
| Current state | "What version of ColdFusion?" |
| | "How is the application deployed?" |
| Pain points | "What's broken or missing?" |
| | "What would you change if you could?" |
| Future | "Where do you see the application in 3 years?" |
| | "What capabilities do you need?" |

---

## During the Session

### Practice Active Listening

- Paraphrase what you hear
- Ask clarifying questions
- Note facts vs. opinions
- Summarize at key points

### Practice Whiteboarding

- Draw as they describe
- Ask "did I get this right?"
- Capture decisions made

---

## After the Session

### Documentation Checklist

- [ ] Key decisions made
- [ ] Open questions remaining
- [ ] Risks identified
- [ ] Constraints noted
- [ ] Timeline expectations
- [ ] Budget indicators
- [ ] Next steps agreed

---

## Deliverable

- Completed documentation checklist
- Architecture diagram
- Notes from the session
- Self-reflection: What went well? What to improve?
