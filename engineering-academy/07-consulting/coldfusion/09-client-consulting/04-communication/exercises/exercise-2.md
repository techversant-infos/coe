# Exercise 2: Active Listening Practice

> Practice the listening techniques that build trust and uncover requirements.

---

## Scenario

A client calls about their ColdFusion application. They're frustrated about performance.

**Client says:**
> "The application is so slow! Every morning it takes forever to load. I've told my team about this and they keep saying it's fine, but it's not fine. We paid for this system and it doesn't work."
